# JedsDead's UnBalance Pass

A broad balance pass on almost all weapons and stratagems

Some enemies and effects were adjusted accordingly to not make it feel too easy or broken.

This mod also includes;
- Blunt-Force Mitigation as a free second passive in solo play.

## Mod menu

On your ship or in a mission, press **F7** to open the mod's menu.

### Knockdown mitigation

Set **knockdown mitigation** from 0% (normal knockdown) to 100% (a zero knockdown multiplier).

The value saves when you release the slider, and is used again when you play solo. With other players present,
the mod removes the custom override so co-op uses the regular balance-pass stats.

### Balance pass changes

Every section and entry of the mod's stats is a switch, on by default: turn off a section, a single entry or all
of them together. The projectile swaps the mod's code makes are listed under weapons.

Switches save at once and rewrite the mod's `hellforge-stats.json` to the enabled changes. With the launcher's
live mod changes on they reach the running game at once; otherwise they apply to the next session.
